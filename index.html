The story so far

I studied electronics back home, spent a year building enterprise software at Accenture in Bengaluru, and did a data-science internship along the way. Now I'm doing my Master's in AI and Data Science at Hochschule Furtwangen, in the Black Forest.

What I actually love is the part most tutorials skip: the maths under the model. The gradient that makes it learn, the eigenvector behind PCA, the softmax inside attention. Away from the screen it's chess, Manchester United most weekends, following RCB through every IPL, and usually a film on while I work. This site keeps me honest. The portfolio's on one side, the daily log on the other, and I don't get to fake the log.
